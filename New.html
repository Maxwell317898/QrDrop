How it works
▼
1
Sender picks a file — it's split into small chunks and encoded as a series of QR codes.
2
QR codes are displayed one by one, auto-advancing. Each encodes a chunk + metadata.
3
Receiver scans each QR using their camera on this page. Duplicates are ignored automatically.
4
File is reassembled and downloaded — no internet, no servers, no accounts needed.
